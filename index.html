HOW TO PLAY

ARROWS / WASD to steer. Auto gas is ON. Hold LEFT or RIGHT into a turn to DRIFT, release for a MINI TURBO.

SPACE uses your charm power. P pauses. M mutes.

Finish TOP 4 to hold the run together. 5th or worse costs you a heart. Zero hearts ends the run.

Your kart has 5 slots: ENGINE, TIRES, BODY, SPOILER, CHARM. Parts raise SPEED, ACCEL, HANDLING, TURBO and WEIGHT, and many carry effects: flame trails, oil slicks, zap attacks, shields, coin magnets.

Match part families for SYNERGY: 2 of a kind is nice, 3 or more is a build. PYRO burns, VOLT charges, GRIP grips, AERO flies, BULK shoves, LUCKY pays.

Later circuits mean faster rivals and meaner tracks, but better parts in the shop. Greed early or scale late. Your call.
