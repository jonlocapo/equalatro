HOW TO PLAY

25 karts, ONE long road, 9 segments. Everyone finishes each segment, THEN the slowest are cut.

ARROWS / WASD to steer. Auto gas is ON. Hold steer into turns to DRIFT, release for MINI TURBO.

SPACE fires your WEAPON. C fires your charm power. E pulls into the PIT. P pauses. M mutes.

WATCH HP AND FUEL. Wrecked karts crawl and regen, stopped karts regen much faster. Empty tanks sputter and refill. Regen booster effects make burn and rebuild a real strategy.

PIT STOPS ARE LIVE. The race never waits. Refuel, repair, bolt on parts, reroll the shop, then launch. Every second costs positions.

Grab WEAPONS from purple cells: homers, mines, static, sugar rush, bubbles, snacks. Back markers get the spicy ones.

Every 3rd segment brings an EVENT: meteor showers, fog banks, gold rushes and worse.

CPU rivals have hidden BOSS effects and driving personalities. The ACE has an unfair FINISHER. Outclass them with lines or loot.

Parts come in Balatro style editions: Foil, Holographic, Polychrome and the mythical free Negative. Strong effects stay rare.
