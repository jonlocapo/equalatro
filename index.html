HOW TO PLAY

25 karts start. After each leg the slowest are CUT. Survive all 4 cuts, then win the 6 kart final.

ARROWS / WASD to steer. Auto gas is ON. Hold LEFT or RIGHT into a turn to DRIFT, release for a MINI TURBO.

SPACE uses your charm power. P pauses. M mutes.

WATCH HP AND FUEL. Hits drain HP. Zero HP taps you out. Fuel drains as you drive and boost. Empty tank sputters. At each PIT STOP choose: free REFUEL or free REPAIR, the other costs 40c. Also grab one part, fast, while rivals grab theirs.

Your kart has 5 slots: ENGINE, TIRES, BODY, SPOILER, CHARM. Match families for SYNERGY: 2 of a kind is nice, 3 or more is a build.

CPU rivals carry HIDDEN BOSS effects, ranked ACE, RIVAL, HUNTER, PACK. The ACE has a FINISHER for the final leg. They pick parts fast but fair. Outdrive them.

The longer the tour runs, the crazier it gets. Fallen rivals feed the loot pool.
