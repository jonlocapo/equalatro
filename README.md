EQUALATRO - a roguelite kart racer in the browser. Draft parts, stack synergies, survive the ladder. Play it with any static server or open index.html directly. No build step. No dependencies. THE LOOP: pick a driver, fill 5 kart slots (ENGINE, TIRES, BODY, SPOILER, CHARM), race 8 karts per circuit, finish top 4 to protect 3 hearts, draft parts and shop between races, climb while rivals and tracks get meaner. PARTS AND SYNERGIES: every part shifts SPEED, ACCEL, HANDLING, TURBO, WEIGHT and many carry effects like flame trails, oil slicks, zap attacks, shields, coin magnets and on demand boosts. Families: PYRO, VOLT, GRIP, AERO, BULK, LUCKY. Two of a family is a rookie bonus, three or more is a champion bonus. CONTROLS: arrows or WASD to steer, gas is automatic, hold steer into turns to drift and release for mini turbo, SPACE fires charm power, P pauses, M mutes, touch buttons on mobile. FILES: index.html screens and HUD, css/style.css UI, js/util.js RNG helpers, js/data.js drivers and 35 parts, js/trackgen.js circuits, js/sim.js headless race sim, js/render.js pseudo 3D road renderer, js/sprites.js pixel art, js/audio.js synth SFX, js/game.js run state and shop, tests/smoke.mjs headless tests. BALANCE: starting karts are slow on purpose, invest or fall behind.
